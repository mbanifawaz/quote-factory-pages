Quote Factory

Privacy Policy

Last updated 2 November 2026

Quote Factory is a personal, self-hosted tool that renders bilingual quote videos and publishes them to a single YouTube channel owned by its operator. It runs on the operator's own computer. It is not a service offered to other people, and it has no other users.

What it accesses

With the operator's own Google account, and only after that account grants permission, the application uses the YouTube Data and YouTube Analytics APIs to:

  • upload videos to that account's own channel;
  • read back the titles, descriptions, tags and statistics of videos on that channel, so the application can show how they performed.

What it stores, and where

Everything stays on the operator's computer: the quotes, the rendered videos, the publishing schedule, and the Google authorisation token that permits the two things above. Nothing is sent anywhere else, and there is no server, hosted database or analytics service behind this application.

What it does not do

  • It collects no data from visitors to this page.
  • It reads no other Google service, and no account other than the operator's own.
  • It shares, sells and transfers no data to anyone. There is nobody to share it with.

Withdrawing access

The operator can revoke the application's access at any time from Google Account permissions, which immediately stops it reaching YouTube. The stored token can be deleted from the machine, which has the same effect locally.
