Quote Factory

A quote channel, run from one machine

Quote Factory renders bilingual Arabic and English quote videos and publishes them to a single YouTube channel. It is self-hosted, used by one person, and runs on that person's own computer.

These pages exist because Google requires an OAuth application to publish a privacy policy and terms of service. There is nothing to sign up for here.
