What this is

Quote Factory is a personal, self-hosted application. It renders bilingual quote videos and publishes them to a single YouTube channel owned by its operator, and it runs on the operator's own computer. It is not offered as a service to anyone else, there are no accounts to register, and there is nothing here to sign up for.

Who may use it

Only the operator. The application authorises against the operator's own Google account, and it has no facility for other users.

YouTube

The application uses the YouTube Data and YouTube Analytics APIs. In using it, the operator is bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and by Google's Privacy Policy. Quotes are published with their authors named, and music is published with whatever attribution its licence requires.
